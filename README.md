##  Project Overview
This project is a comprehensive study conducted as part of the **Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI)**. It explores the rising trend of mental health self-diagnosis facilitated by social media platforms. Our research investigates how digital content influences the self-perception of mental health among young adults and the potential risks associated with unverified online medical information.

> **Mission:** To bridge the gap between digital self-diagnosis and professional medical consultation through data analysis and predictive modeling.

---

##  Key Features
| Feature | Implementation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Data Integration** | Analysis of Survey Data (355+ responses), Google Trends (5-year history), and Behavioral Datasets. |
| **Predictive Analytics** | Machine Learning model to identify users at risk of stress and anxiety based on digital behavior. |
| **Interactive Dashboard** | A real-time Streamlit web application for data exploration and model testing. |
| **Strategic Insights** | Identification of the "Consultation Gap" and platform-specific behavioral correlations. |

---

##  Key Findings & Statistics
*   **Demographic Focus:** **83%** of individuals engaging in self-diagnosis are aged **18-24**.
*   **The Consultation Gap:** Only **9.6%** of users seek professional medical advice after self-diagnosing online.
*   **Platform Impact:** **TikTok** and **Instagram** show the strongest correlation with increased stress and anxiety levels.
*   **Emotional Outcome:** Engagement with self-diagnosis content frequently leads to increased **Doubt, Confusion, and Stress** rather than relief.

---

##  Machine Learning Approach 🤖
Our predictive engine follows a structured data science workflow:
1.  **Data Preprocessing:** Cleaning and normalizing multi-source datasets.
2.  **Feature Engineering:** Identifying key behavioral predictors such as platform usage duration and engagement type.
3.  **Model Selection:** Training and validating classification models (e.g., Random Forest) to predict mental states.
4.  **Deployment:** Integrating the model into the Streamlit app for user-friendly accessibility.

##  Recommendations 💡
*   **For Users:** Treat social media content as an awareness tool, not a diagnostic one. Always verify with professionals.
*   **For Platforms:** Implement mandatory disclaimers and link users to verified mental health resources.
*   **For Healthcare:** Increase digital outreach to provide evidence-based mental health education.

> **Project:** Self-Diagnosis on Social Media: Trends, Risks, and Behavioral Analysis  
> **Goal:** Examine how social media users self-diagnose mental health conditions, analyze trends, engagement patterns, and identify potential risks.

---

## Problem Statement
Social media platforms such as **TikTok** and **Twitter** have experienced a surge in mental health-related content. Users often **self-diagnose conditions** like ADHD, anxiety, and depression based on short-form content without consulting professionals.

**Impacts include:**
- ⚠️ Spread of **misinformation**  
- Increased **stress and anxiety**  
- Risk of **inappropriate self-treatment**

## Project Goals
The project focuses on **analyzing trends, engagement, and risks** of self-diagnosis content using data analysis and machine learning. Key objectives:

- Identify **common self-diagnosis topics**  
- Detect **misinformation vs verified content**  
- Analyze **user sentiment and engagement trends**  
- Forecast future trends to support **awareness and prevention**

---

## Approach

### 1. Data Collection & Preprocessing
- **Sources:** Twitter, TikTok hashtags, Google Trends  
- **Content:** Text, engagement metrics, hashtags, timestamps  
- **Preprocessing:** Noise removal, tokenization, lemmatization, handling missing values  
- **Tools:** Python (`pandas`, `NLTK`, `spaCy`), SQL  

**Deliverables:** Clean dataset and preprocessing notebook

---

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis
- **Keyword Analysis:** Most frequent words and hashtags  
- **Sentiment Analysis:** Positive, negative, neutral trends  
- **Temporal Trends:** Daily, weekly, monthly patterns  
- **Visualization:** Word clouds, hashtag clusters, descriptive statistics  
- **Tools:** Python (`matplotlib`, `seaborn`, `pandas`, NLP libraries)  

**Deliverables:** Charts, graphs, and insights summary

---

### 3. Deep Dive & Forecasting
- **Classification:** Scientific/verified vs misleading content  
- **User Segmentation:** Influencers, experts, general users  
- **Correlation Analysis:** Engagement vs sentiment/misinformation  
- **Forecasting:** Time series and regression models  
- **Visualization:** Heatmaps, topic clusters, forecast plots  
- **Tools:** Python (`scikit-learn`, `statsmodels`)  

**Deliverables:** Analysis report and forecast visualizations

---

### 4. Dashboard & Presentation
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Filters by platform, sentiment, keyword, and date  
- **Visualizations:** Trend lines, engagement metrics, sentiment distribution  
- **Report & Slides:** Summarize key insights, risks, and recommendations  
- **Tools:** Tableau, PowerPoint  

**Deliverables:** Interactive dashboard, final report, and presentation slides

---

## Why This Matters
Analyzing social media self-diagnosis trends helps:

- 🧠 Users make **informed decisions**  
- Mental health professionals **understand emerging patterns**  
- Platforms **moderate and flag misleading content** effectively
